# KPI Glossary

## Total Runs
Sum of TOTAL_RUNS from FACT_DELIVERY.

## Wickets
Sum of IS_WICKET from FACT_DELIVERY.

## Run Rate
Total runs divided by balls, multiplied by 6 for the displayed per-over rate.

## Boundary %
(Fours + Sixes) divided by balls multiplied by 100.

## Dot Ball %
Dot balls divided by balls multiplied by 100.

## Extras
Wides + no-balls + byes + leg-byes.
